The valuation and the shadow dispatch now follow methodology v3.2 and dispatch rules v1.4. The desk write-up is in `POSTMORTEM.md`.

**Valuation.** Five defects were pricing the October book as if the hurricane would raise Metro prices. Hub-height wind (`wind_100m`) replaces the surface reading. A hurricane or freeze with an outage fraction of at least 0.15 inverts that region’s bias and drops the demand term. Path congestion uses source minus sink shift factors. An import limit into a knocked-out region has binding probability 0 in that scenario. Options pay `p * max(0, spread)` in each scenario, and only the final value is rounded to the cent. September path P01 is the worked example, **10.62**. November is in `out/valuation_2026-11.csv` (P01 obligation 1.75 through P10 option 25.18).

October’s H-1026 spreads match Risk to the cent on every path except **P07**. The engine’s **5.10** is the methodology spread: baseline 1.70, weather 3.76, PAN_NORTH −0.36, and METRO_IMPORT excluded because Metro is knocked out. Risk’s **7.94** is 5.10 plus that slack import term, `(0.18 − (−0.25)) * 12.00 * 0.55 = 2.838`, which rounds 7.938 to 7.94. The other paths with a non-zero METRO_IMPORT exposure match Risk only with that term removed.

**Dispatch.** Each hour is a security-constrained linear program: offer cost plus $9,000/MWh of shed, power balance, unit and wind availability from the hub-height curve, and both flow directions inside the SCADA post-contingency limit of each monitored element that is in service. `SOUTH_NORTH_2` stays informational. The 17 November dispatch is in `out/dispatch_2026-11-17.csv`: production cost **$1,536,059.91**, no load shed. Night hours run both wind units at rated output.

**Backtest.** `BACKTEST FAIL 2026-11-10: max |LMP error| $85.1944, MAE $9.6626; 38 price breaches, 3 binding mismatches in 24 hours`. Hours 0–7, 10–16, and 20–23 match PGO to the cent. Hours 8 and 9 are published as a flat $140.00 and $137.78, which is the stack only if 900 MW of capacity offered below $120 is absent and nothing binds. Three full-unit outages fit that stack exactly (the nuke, or South gas plus West gas, or South coal plus West gas), and none of them is in the feeds. With every thermal and the nuke available, both hours bind the North import at the SCADA limit of 820 MW. Hours 17–19 were priced as if that limit were still 820 MW. SCADA de-rates it to 600 MW from 17:00, and hour 18 has the same load, wind, and limits as hour 20, which the model matches (North import shadow 125). The engine was not changed to force those five hours.
