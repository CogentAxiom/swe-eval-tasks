# Amendment No. 3 to Credit Agreement

*Dated as of June 24, 2026, among Larkspur Provisions, Inc. (the "Borrower"), the Lenders party
hereto, and Coldwater National Bank, N.A., as Administrative Agent (the "Agent"). All parties are
fictional.*

**RECITALS.** The Borrower, the Lenders and the Agent are parties to the Credit Agreement dated as of
March 14, 2024 (as amended by Amendment No. 1 and Amendment No. 2, the "Credit Agreement"). The
Borrower has requested, and the Lenders have agreed, to amend the Credit Agreement as set forth below.

## Section 1. Defined Terms

Capitalized terms used and not defined herein have the meanings given to them in the Credit Agreement.

## Section 2. Amendments to the Credit Agreement

Subject to Section 3, the Credit Agreement is hereby amended as follows:

**(a)** Clause (a) of the definition of "Eligible Accounts" in Section 1.01 is amended and restated in
its entirety to read as follows:

> "(a) Accounts unpaid more than ninety (90) days after the original invoice date or more than sixty
> (60) days after the original due date;"

**(b)** Clause (b) of the definition of "Eligible Accounts" in Section 1.01 is amended by replacing
"fifty percent (50%)" with "thirty-five percent (35%)".

**(c)** Clause (f) of the definition of "Eligible Accounts" in Section 1.01 is amended by replacing
"twenty percent (20%)" with "twenty percent (20%) (or, in the case of Accounts owing by an Investment
Grade Account Debtor, thirty-five percent (35%))".

**(d)** The definition of "Eligible Accounts" in Section 1.01 is further amended by deleting the word
"and" at the end of clause (e), replacing the period at the end of clause (f) with "; and", and
inserting the following new clause (g) immediately following clause (f):

> "(g) Accounts arising out of a bill-and-hold, guaranteed sale, sale-or-return, sale on approval,
> consignment or progress billing arrangement."

**(e)** Clause (b) of the definition of "Borrowing Base" in Section 1.01 is amended and restated in its
entirety to read as follows:

> "(b) the least of (i) fifty-five percent (55%) of Eligible Inventory, (ii) eighty-five percent (85%)
> of the NOLV Percentage multiplied by Eligible Inventory and (iii) $4,000,000; *minus*"

**(f)** The definition of "Reserves" in Section 1.01 is amended by adding the following at the end
thereof: "Reserves shall in any event include the Dilution Reserve."

**(g)** Section 1.01 is amended by adding the following new definitions in the appropriate
alphabetical order:

> **"Dilution Ratio"** means, as of any date of determination, the ratio (expressed as a decimal) of
> (a) non-cash credits to Accounts (including credit memos, discounts, allowances and write-offs)
> during the twelve fiscal months most recently ended to (b) gross sales during such period, as most
> recently determined by the Agent in a field examination and notified in writing to the Borrower.
>
> **"Dilution Reserve"** means, as of any date of determination, an amount equal to the product of
> (a) Eligible Accounts *multiplied by* (b) the amount, if any, by which the Dilution Ratio exceeds
> five hundredths (0.05).
>
> **"Investment Grade Account Debtor"** means each Account Debtor listed on Schedule 1.01(c), for so
> long as such Account Debtor's long-term issuer credit rating from S&P is BBB- or higher. An Account
> Debtor listed on Schedule 1.01(c) that has no such rating is not an Investment Grade Account Debtor.
>
> **"original due date"** means, with respect to any Account, the date on which payment of such
> Account is due under the payment terms stated on the original invoice, without giving effect to any
> extension, rebilling, or other modification of such terms.

## Section 3. Effectiveness

This Amendment shall become effective on July 1, 2026 (the "Amendment Effective Date"). The amendments
set forth in Section 2 shall apply to the computation of the Borrowing Base and Availability as of the
last day of each fiscal month ending on or after the Amendment Effective Date. The Credit Agreement as
in effect immediately prior to the Amendment Effective Date shall continue to govern any such
computation as of an earlier date, including any Borrowing Base Certificate re-delivered after the
Amendment Effective Date with respect to a fiscal month ending before the Amendment Effective Date;
*provided* that the Dilution Reserve shall be included in Reserves only for fiscal months ending on or
after September 30, 2026.

## Section 4. Representations; Reference to and Effect on the Credit Agreement

Except as expressly amended hereby, the Credit Agreement remains in full force and effect. Each
reference in the Credit Agreement to "this Agreement" shall mean the Credit Agreement as amended hereby.
